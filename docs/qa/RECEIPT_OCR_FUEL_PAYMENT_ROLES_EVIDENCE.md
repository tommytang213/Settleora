# Fuel and payment panel ownership

A source-verified fuel receipt already preserves its fuel item and financial fields but incorrectly treats the pump identifier, payment heading and closing courtesy as unresolved item evidence. A synthetic two-digit pump also displaces the valid fuel item. Both defects concern provisional OCR row roles.

A pump identifier is owned only inside five adjacent, aligned two-column rows containing the pump, product, volume, unit rate and selected transaction total. Require a positive one-to-four-digit identifier, the existing extracted fuel description and total, separate horizontally ordered columns, vertical overlap, bounded row spacing and finite convex geometry with confidence at least 0.8. Incomplete panels and priced pump evidence remain reviewable.

A separate closing-payment proof requires the selected total immediately before a complete payment-method heading, recognized card with last four digits, approved authorization pair and two complete courtesy phrases at the end. Require bounded geometry and spacing, aligned payment fields, centered contained closing copy and no competing nearby cells from any earlier provider row. The proof owns only the heading and courtesy rows. Card, authorization and all original raw blocks remain preserved.

Fifty-six synthetic controls cover one- and two-digit pumps, leading zeros and the four-digit bound, gallon/liter variants, scale and translation, partial panels, priced pump purchases, separate tip evidence, independent service-label ambiguity, detached footer prices on either side with earlier provider row IDs, overlapping or vertically separated columns, distant payment fields, missing or invalid confidence and invalid polygons. Existing courtesy and payment identity controls also pass. Initial tests exposed a merchant service-label ambiguity and an incorrect expectation for an already retained tip; those controls retain the actual independent review condition. Geometry counterexamples were fixed before final validation.

A private replay compares 41 preview fields on 83 retained snapshots spanning 48 fixture IDs. Only the fuel snapshot changes row/completeness/review state; all amounts, item candidates and original blocks remain identical on every snapshot. Strict-complete snapshots increase from 35 to 36, mismatches decrease from 138 to 137 and review-hint snapshots decrease from 35 to 34. Ordinary-warning snapshots remain 40. Repeated historical captures are not fresh native acceptance.

This five-file delta changes parser roles, synthetic tests, this evidence note and parser-bound catalog hashes. Fixture truth/images, model/native code, preview/save/Apply authority, API/schema, money calculations, security/access, dependencies/workflows and release state are unchanged. Full exact-head validation, both existing independent reviews, source-bound package/native evidence and final human approval remain required.
