The parser recognizes a complete Portuguese service label, optionally qualified as a service fee or followed by one bounded percentage, with exactly one monetary cell. Matching is case-insensitive and supports the standard cedilla or its ASCII transcription. A colon or whitespace separates the label from the cell. Capturing the entire cell preserves attached currency codes and the exact amount token. It does not infer an amount from the percentage or arithmetic.

Product qualifiers, extra suffixes, multiple prices and incomplete rate syntax are not consumed as service charges. Potential service evidence remains reviewable when the role is incomplete. Foreign, conflicting and unresolved currencies continue through the existing provisional-save guards.
